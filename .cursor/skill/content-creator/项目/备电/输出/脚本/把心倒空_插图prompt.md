# 把心倒空 — 插图 Image2 提示词

> 长文配图 | 2张 | 封面 + 文中情绪高峰
> 视觉基调：温暖、人情味、积极。两张同属一个视觉世界。

---

## 图1 · 封面

**位置**：文章顶部

**任务**：两句判断砸下来，不留余地。但画面本身是温情的——判断的冷和画面的暖形成张力。

```
A 9:16 vertical image (1080x1920px). ALL on-screen text must be in CHINESE.
This is the header image for a warm, personal essay about emotional bookkeeping —
the quiet ledgers we all keep without meaning to.
The visual mood is gentle, human, intimate. Like a friend sitting across the table.
But the title is not gentle. It is a verdict. Two sentences. No softening.
First line, large and bold: "账本不是关系。"
Second line, equally bold, the final word: "账本是关系的尽头。"
The contrast is the point: a warm, intimate image carrying a cold, hard truth.
The reader should feel both at once — the sting of recognition and the warmth
of being understood. No subtitles, no CTA, no decorative elements.
Content safe zone: 120px to 1750px from top.
```

## 图2 · 文中情绪高峰

**位置**：林黛玉 + 琼瑶段落之后（「连写爱情的人也算不清」的下方）

**任务**：读者刚读完两个女人一生的故事。这里不需要再证明什么——需要的是被理解。一张图让读者喘口气，感受到这件事不是他一个人的。

```
A 9:16 vertical image (1080x1920px). ALL on-screen text must be in CHINESE.
Same visual world as the cover — same warmth, same intimacy.
This image appears at a turning point in the essay. The reader has just read about
two women who spent their lives keeping emotional accounts that were never settled.
The essay is about to say: this isn't your fault. The world taught us to keep score.
But we can choose to stop.
The visual should feel like a breath. A pause. Someone put a hand on your shoulder
and said nothing — just stayed there for a moment.
It is not sad. It is warm. It carries the feeling of: you're not alone in this.
No text required — the image is the feeling between the words.
Content safe zone: 120px to 1750px from top.
```
