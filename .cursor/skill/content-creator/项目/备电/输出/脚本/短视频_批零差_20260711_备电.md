## 导演分析摘要

```
主线：小周拿着白菜问储能 → 增元用王大姐冷库讲批零差 → 小周自己悟出来"就是那个冷库！"
钩子：女生先出镜。"哎增元，那个储能电池。放那儿一年赚十几万，它到底赚的什么钱啊？"
      小周+菜市场+白菜=视觉抓眼。女生先出镜天然更高留存。
情绪：好奇 → 认真听 → 被问住 → 自己悟出来 → 得意
核心手法：概念名"批零差"开场就说了。但观众要追到镜5才真正懂这个词的重量。
```

```
小周：一块电池一年十几万。
      赚的什么钱？

      菜市场的生意。
```

She's holding a white cabbage in one hand, looking slightly off-camera toward Zengyuan with genuine curiosity. Behind her, vegetable stalls with green bok choy and stacked cabbages, soft golden morning sunlight, other shoppers blurred into warm bokeh. When Zengyuan's voice answers "批零差。菜市场的生意。" from off-screen, her expression shifts — eyes widen slightly, the answer is not what she expected. 9:16 vertical. Medium close-up, chest up. Camera fixed. Morning market ambience, light guitar starts.

### 镜3：边走边讲·冷库的坑（14-30s）

**【口播台词】**
```
小周：等到下午5点，
      小区门口一斤1块嘛。
      这不就低买高卖。

增元：那她租冷库干嘛？

小周：不租冷库……
      白菜到下午就烂了呀。

增元：所以冷库赚的什么钱？
```

**【即梦 prompt】**
Zengyuan and Xiao Zhou walking slowly side by side through the market aisle. Zengyuan in dark grey athletic t-shirt on the left, Xiao Zhou in white athletic tank top, ponytail on the right. Morning sun through awning creates warm dappled light on them. As Zengyuan talks he takes the cabbage from Xiao Zhou's hand naturally — holding it up when explaining "冷库" as if the cabbage represents the goods being stored. Xiao Zhou walks with hands now free, gesturing confidently when she finishes his sentence — she knows this part. When Zengyuan asks "冷库赚的是什么钱" he stops walking, turns to face her. Xiao Zhou stops too, her confident expression freezes — she doesn't have the answer. The cabbage in Zengyuan's hand is the silent question between them. Market bustle continues around them, a vendor's call echoes. Guitar continues, the light beat drops out on the question — a tiny pocket of silence. 9:16 vertical. Medium two-shot, slight handheld tracking feel. Warm morning light.

**【连续性】**

### 镜4：翻译时刻·小周悟了（30-43s）

**【口播台词】**
```
小周：冷库赚的……不是白菜的钱。
      是时间。
      让白菜能扛到下午。
      3毛变1块，
      中间是时间在赚钱。

增元：对。那把电当成白菜。
      晚上3毛3。

小周：早上的白菜。

增元：白天1块1。

小周：下午的白菜。
      那储能柜不就是……

增元：嗯？

小周：就是那个冷库啊！！
```

**【即梦 prompt】**
Two-shot, they've stopped walking, standing facing each other by a vegetable stall. Zengyuan in dark grey athletic t-shirt, Xiao Zhou in white athletic tank top, ponytail. Xiao Zhou is staring at the cabbage in Zengyuan's hand — thinking out loud, her voice slower, putting pieces together. When she says "时间的钱" her eyes lift to meet his — she just discovered something. Zengyuan gives one short nod. Then he hands the cabbage back to her when he says "当成白菜." Xiao Zhou takes it, now looking at it completely differently. Zengyuan's pace quickens through "3毛3, 1块1" — building momentum, each number a step closer. He pauses deliberately before "储能柜——" leaving the space open. Xiao Zhou's face lights up, ponytail bouncing as she almost jumps — "就是那个冷库!!" — she figured it out before he could finish. She says it loud, the cabbage now held up like a trophy. 9:16 vertical. Medium two-shot, slightly tighter than shot 3. Market background softly blurred. Morning light unchanged. Guitar builds subtly through the numbers, a small drum fill lands exactly on "冷库!!" — then everything drops to just guitar for the satisfaction moment.

**【连续性】**
transition: 硬切
carryOver: 白菜在增元手里。两人面对面停下来。镜3的问题"冷库赚什么钱"在这里被小周自己回答。
newElements: 白菜从增元还给小周——答案也跟着过去了。小周抢答"就是那个冷库！！"——翻译不是增元做的，是小周替观众完成的。
emotionLink: 被问住 → 自己想通 → 兴奋（"就是那个冷库！！我懂了！！"）

**【audienceMap】**
| 眼睛 | 心里 |

| 增元把白菜还给她 | 答案在她手里了 |
