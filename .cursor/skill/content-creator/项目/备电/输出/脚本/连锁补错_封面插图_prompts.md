## 封面

任务：让刷到的人停下来。锚点 + 悬念，≤3 样。不给答案不给结论。

封面文字：
- 主标题：**明知道该停，为什么停不下来**
- 悬念行：烂片坐了俩小时。亏了加仓。分手拖了三年。不是不懂。是人性。

```
9:16 vertical image, 1080x1920px. ALL on-screen text must be in CHINESE.

This is the cover of a short video about behavioral economics and
self-deception. The visual should create immediate recognition — the
viewer sees themselves in it.

Large bold Chinese title text,占据画面中央偏上: "明知道该停，为什么停不下来".
The title characters are warm white on dark ground, with a single keyword
"停不下来" highlighted in amber color block — like a highlighter stroke.

Below it, a smaller suspense line: "烂片坐了俩小时。亏了加仓。分手拖了三年。不是不懂。是人性。"

The background visual: a single wooden domino standing upright on a dark
surface. Behind it, a chain of identical dominoes already fallen, curving
into shadow. A warm theater-spotlight beam hits the standing domino from
above. Deep charcoal void beyond.

The domino image is atmospheric, not literal product photography — the
viewer senses "chain reaction" and "the one still standing" without
needing to analyze it.

Three elements only: title, suspense line, domino visual. No conclusions,
no golden quotes, no CTA. Key content within 120–1750px safe zone.
```

## 插图 2 · 四问

出现时机：口播 P7 段 "下次那个声音从脑子里冒出来的时候。问自己四句。"

画面文字（画面中央，卡片上）：
- **连锁补错四问**
- ① 在解决问题，还是在保面子？
- ② 如果今天才刚开始，会做这个选择吗？
- ③ 停下来最坏 vs 继续下去最坏，哪个更承受不了？
- ④ 在给谁证明？证明什么？

```
9:16 vertical image, 1080x1920px. ALL on-screen text must be in CHINESE.

A dark wooden desk seen from above at a slight angle. Four small cards
or memo notes are arranged in a column on the desk surface — slightly
overlapping, each positioned a bit lower than the one above, like steps
going down.

Each card is warm cream-colored, handwritten in dark ink. The text on
each card is a single question in Chinese:

Card 1: "在解决问题，还是在保面子？"
Card 2: "如果今天才刚开始，会做这个选择吗？"
Card 3: "停下来最坏 vs 继续下去最坏？"
Card 4: "在给谁证明？证明什么？"

At the top of the column, a small header card in amber-tinted paper:
"连锁补错四问"

A warm desk lamp from upper left casts amber light across the cards.
The lamp body is just barely visible at the edge of the frame. Deep
charcoal shadow beyond the desk. A black fountain pen rests beside
the cards, uncapped — ready to write.

The questions are the focal point. The viewer should want to pause
and read all four.
```

## 使用节奏

```
口播开始 → 封面作为视频缩略图（观众点进来前看到）
前 3 分钟 → 讲者出镜，无插图
P4 一文钱段 → 插图1 切入，停留 6-8 秒，切回讲者
P7 四问段   → 插图2 切入，停留到最后，配合四问逐条念出
```
